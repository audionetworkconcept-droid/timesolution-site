Auto-Setup (Sweep / IR)

How It Works

Auto-Setup runs a Farina sweep through the wedge/monitor output. FeedZero captures the response via the stage mic. Calculates the IR (impulse response) of the system. Suggests notches and starting parameters adapted to your rig.

What It Is / What It Isn't

💡

Auto-Setup is a configuration aid, not a magic solution. Results depend on mic placement quality and acoustic conditions at the time of the sweep.

Procedure

  1. Position the mic in the actual performance position
  2. Mute all audio sources (musicians, other wedges)
  3. Launch Auto-Setup from the FeedZero interface
  4. Wait for the sweep to complete (a few seconds)
  5. Validate or adjust the suggested parameters

Warnings

⚠️

The sweep is audible. Alert anyone in the room. Don't move the mic during the sweep.

🚫

Do not run Auto-Setup during a live event. Reserve it for soundcheck.

After the Sweep

The suggested parameters are a starting point. Refine them based on real-world results. Re-run Auto-Setup if you significantly change the mic placement or levels.
