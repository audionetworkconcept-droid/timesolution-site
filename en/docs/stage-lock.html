Stage Lock

What is Stage Lock?

A stable, adaptive suppression mode. FeedZero places notches progressively as feedback risk increases. Sound transparency is the priority. Ideal for long sets and controlled situations.

Behavior

  • Feedback detection via real-time spectral analysis
  • Narrow precise notches placed at problem frequencies
  • Progressive notch removal as risk decreases
  • No mute, no service interruption

When to Use It

  • Concerts, conferences, events with extended speech sections
  • Situations where sound coloring is unacceptable
  • When the situation is under control and you can manage gradually

Limitations

⚠️

Stage Lock won't stop violent feedback instantly. For emergencies, use KILL or PANIC.

Stage Lock is optimized for stability. It may allow a few milliseconds of pre-feedback before acting.
